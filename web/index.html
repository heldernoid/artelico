a.Artelico

Small models on any device

Artelico is an experiment in running computer vision, and small speech-to-text, language and vision-language models, on any device: a web browser, a phone or tablet, a laptop, or a small USB accelerator. It is open source. This site is the browser version.

What is here

  • Photos and Live: about 200 models for detection, pose, segmentation, depth, faces, text and more, on a picture, the camera, a video file or a road camera.
  • Small apps built on them: pose and hand games, counting people and cars, number plates, face check-in, a privacy camera that blurs faces and plates.
  • Speech and language: speech to text (Whisper), chat with small language models, and questions about a picture with small vision-language models.

How it works

The models come from the Hailo Model Zoo, converted to run in a browser (ONNX Runtime Web, on the graphics chip with WebGPU, or on the processor with WebAssembly). Each one downloads the first time it is used and is kept by the browser for next time. Most vision models are under 50 MB; language models are larger.

Everything runs in this browser. Pictures, the camera and what you type stay on this computer; only the models are downloaded.

Other ways to run it

  • Artelico, an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, runs the same models with Core ML.
  • Hailo Studio runs them on a Hailo-10 USB accelerator connected to an iPad or Mac.

Both are built from the source code.

Links

  • Source code: github.com/heldernoid/artelico (AGPL-3.0)
  • Models: huggingface.co/helmo/artelico-models

Works best in a recent version of Chrome, Edge or Safari. A computer with a graphics chip is faster.
